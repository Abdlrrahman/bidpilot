# 90-Second Walkthrough Demo Script

## Audience: Leadership, Hiring Managers & Procurement Evaluators

---

### [0:00 - 0:15] Hook & Opportunity Overview
- **Action:** Open the **Overview** dashboard.
- **Script:** *"Welcome to BidPilot. This is an explainable bid-readiness control tower built for suppliers competing on institutional and donor tenders. Here we see a pre-seeded $485,000 Municipal IT Infrastructure opportunity in Libya. Notice our key KPIs: 90.4% weighted compliance, 16.3% gross margin, and a positive statistical Expected Bid Value of $37,845."*

---

### [0:15 - 0:35] Interactive Compliance & The Hard Blocker Rule
- **Action:** Click on the **Compliance Matrix** tab. Search for `TECH-01` (Enterprise Server). Change its compliance state from **Comply** to **Gap**.
- **Script:** *"Let's test our compliance engine. If we mark this mandatory server requirement as a 'Gap', notice how our header badge immediately turns red and triggers a high-severity NO-GO blocker. Even with 85%+ overall coverage, institutional rules disqualify non-compliant tenders. BidPilot prevents costly bidding on disqualified opportunities."*
- **Action:** Switch `TECH-01` back to **Comply**.

---

### [0:35 - 0:55] Landed Cost Waterfall & Multi-Scenario Margin
- **Action:** Navigate to the **Pricing & Waterfall** tab. Switch between **Conservative**, **Target**, and **Competitive** scenarios.
- **Script:** *"In the Pricing lab, we calculate full landed costs including direct FOB purchases, international freight, port clearance, and contingency reserves. We compare three distinct pricing strategies side-by-side to ensure our proposed revenue remains within the buyer's budget ceiling while safeguarding our profit margin."*

---

### [0:55 - 1:15] Explainable Go/No-Go Decision Gate
- **Action:** Navigate to the **Go / No-Go Decision** tab.
- **Script:** *"The Go/No-Go decision gate is completely explainable. It evaluates 6 weighted dimensions—compliance, delivery feasibility, margin, strategic fit, evidence completeness, and pursuit ROI. Every factor displays its raw score, weighted contribution, and plain-language explanation."*

---

### [1:15 - 1:30] Bilingual RTL & Executive Print Brief
- **Action:** Click **العربية (RTL)** in the header to show instant Arabic layout. Navigate to the **Executive Brief** tab and show the print-ready one-page report.
- **Script:** *"BidPilot fully supports bidirectional English and Arabic RTL workflows. Finally, the Executive Brief compiles our entire opportunity analysis, modeled time savings, and next steps into a print-ready briefing document."*
